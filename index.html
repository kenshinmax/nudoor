FAQ

Common questions

Do you ever see or store our customers' card data?

No. We use Stripe-hosted payment fields (Checkout, Elements, Terminal), so card data goes straight to Stripe. That keeps you in the lightest PCI scope.

Who owns the Stripe account?

You do, always. We work with restricted, revocable access and hand over full documentation at the end.

Do you work with Shopify, WooCommerce, or custom apps?

Yes. We work with custom web and mobile apps, popular commerce platforms, and the accounting and CRM tools you already use.

How fast can we get started?

Usually within two weeks of signing. Health Checks start within three business days.

Are you affiliated with Stripe?

We're an independent company that builds on Stripe. [After acceptance: "We're a member of the Stripe Partner Ecosystem."]
